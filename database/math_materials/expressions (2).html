01 Formulas
Combining like terms
\(2x^2+3x^2=5x^2\)
\(x^2+x \neq x^3\)
FOIL method
\((a+b)(c+d)=ac+ad+bc+bd\)
\((x+2)(x+3)=x^2+5x+6\)
Square of a sum
\((a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2\)
\((x+3)^2=x^2+6x+9\)
Square of a difference
\((a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2\)
\((x-4)^2=x^2-8x+16\)
Difference of squares
\(a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)\)
\(x^2-9=(x+3)(x-3)\)
GCF factoring
\(ax+ay=a(x+y)\)
\(6x+9=3(2x+3)\)
Adding fractions
\(\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{c}{d}=\dfrac{ad+bc}{bd}\)
\(\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{2+x}{2x}\)
Dividing fractions
\(\dfrac{\,a/b\,}{c/d}=\dfrac{a}{b}\cdot\dfrac{d}{c}\)
\(\dfrac{2/x}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2x}\)
Splitting fractions
\(\dfrac{a+b}{c}=\dfrac{a}{c}+\dfrac{b}{c}\)
\(\dfrac{c}{a+b} \neq \dfrac{c}{a}+\dfrac{c}{b}\)
