SAT MATH · CHAPTER 11 REV. 01

Word Problems

Setups · Translation · Practice

Word problems don't test a brand-new formula — they test whether you can turn a sentence into an equation. Learn the ten setups that cover almost every scenario, master the phrase-to-math dictionary, work through six full solutions, then climb the ten-question ladder: three easy, three medium, three hard, and one true extreme.

01 Formulas
Common Problem-Solving Setups
Fixed + variable cost
\(\text{Total} = \text{Fixed} + (\text{Rate})(\text{Quantity})\)
\(c = 45 + 30m\)
Distance, rate, time
\(d = rt\)
\(d = 60t\)
Consecutive integers
\(x,\ x{+}1,\ x{+}2,\ \dots\)
sum of 3: \(x+(x{+}1)+(x{+}2)\)
Percent of a whole
\(\text{part} = p\cdot(\text{whole})\)
\(0.70x\) attend full-time
Age in y years
\(\text{future age} = \text{current age} + y\)
in 5 years: \(x+5\)
Ratio / proportion
\(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d} \Rightarrow ad = bc\)
\(\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{6}{k} \Rightarrow k=4\)
Combined work rate
\(\dfrac{1}{t_A}+\dfrac{1}{t_B}=\dfrac{1}{t_{\text{together}}}\)
\(\dfrac18+\dfrac1{12}=\dfrac1t\)
Mixture (adding solutions)
\(c_1v_1+c_2v_2=c_{f}(v_1{+}v_2)\)
\(0.10(30)+0.50x=0.20(30{+}x)\)
Two-rate / piecewise
\(\text{Total}=r_1t_1+r_2(t-t_1),\ t>t_1\)
\(6(2)+4(t-2)=20\)
Part-whole (segments/groups)
\(\text{whole}=\text{part}_1+\text{part}_2\)
\(AB+BC=AC\)

Word-to-Math Dictionary
increased by · more than · sum of · added to\(+\)
decreased by · less than · difference of\(-\)
times as many as · product of · "of"\(\times\)
per · for every · ratio of ___ to ___\(\div\)
is · was · will be · results in · gives\(=\)
⚠ "less than" flips the order:\(5\text{ less than }3x \Rightarrow 3x-5,\ \text{not } 5-3x\)
Assign the variable wisely
let \(x\) = what the question asks for
fewer conversions at the end = fewer mistakes
Unit-conversion chains
\(\text{qty}\times\dfrac{\text{new unit}}{\text{old unit}}\times\cdots\)
multiply by chained fractions so units cancel
Replacement mixture
\(c_1(V{-}x)+c_2x=c_{f}\cdot V\)
draining & refilling keeps total volume \(V\) fixed
Reasonableness check
does the answer fit the story?
a "leftover" can't be negative or bigger than the start
