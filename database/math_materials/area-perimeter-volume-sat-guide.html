01 Formulas
Core Area & Volume Toolkit
Square
\(A=s^2\)   \(P=4s\)
perimeter 52 \(\Rightarrow s=13 \Rightarrow A=169\)
Rectangle
\(A=lw\)   \(P=2l+2w\)
\(l=14,\ w=9 \Rightarrow A=126\)
Parallelogram
\(A=bh\)
any side can be the base — height must be perpendicular to it
Cube & rectangular prism
\(V=lwh\)   \(SA=2lw{+}2lh{+}2wh\)
\(V=\)Base Area\(\times\)height — true for ANY right prism
Right circular cylinder
\(V=\pi r^2h\)   \(SA=2\pi r^2{+}2\pi rh\)
unroll the side: a \(2\pi r \times h\) rectangle
Sphere, cone & pyramid volumes
Sphere: \(V=\frac43\pi r^3\)
Cone: \(V=\frac13\pi r^2h\)
Pyramid: \(V=\frac13 lwh\)
the SAT gives you these — but memorize them anyway
Similar polygons
Area ratio \(=(\text{side ratio})^2\)
sides 5\(\times\) longer \(\Rightarrow\) area \(25\times\) larger
Similar solids
Volume ratio \(=(\text{edge ratio})^3\)
edges 12\(\times\) longer \(\Rightarrow\) volume 1{,}728\(\times\) larger
Percent-change scaling
\(k=1+\dfrac{x\%}{100}\ \Rightarrow\) Area \(\times k^2\), Volume \(\times k^3\)
radius \(+30\% \Rightarrow k=1.30 \Rightarrow\) area \(\times1.69\) (69% greater)
Density
\(\text{Density}=\dfrac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Volume}}\)
126 lb \(\div\) 1.5 ft³ = 84 lb/ft³

Which Formula Do You Need?
Flat 2D shape — need area or perimeter?Square / Rectangle / Parallelogram
3D solid with two identical flat ends?Volume = Base Area × Height
Sphere, cone, or pyramid?Use its dedicated volume formula
Two shapes/solids are similar?Area ×(ratio)² · Volume ×(ratio)³
⚠ A dimension changes by a PERCENT?Convert to a multiplier first — +30% means ×1.30, not ×0.30
Reverse scale-factor logic
Volume ratio \(=k^3 \Rightarrow\) linear ratio \(=\sqrt[3]{k}\)
volume ratio 216 \(\Rightarrow\) radius ratio \(=\sqrt[3]{216}=6\)
Multi-factor scaling
scale each dimension separately, using its own exponent
cone: \(r\to\frac13r,\ h\to3h \Rightarrow\) new \(V=\left(\frac13\right)^2{\cdot}3\times\) old \(V\)
Base × height works for ANY base
\(V=(\text{Area of any base shape})\times h\)
right-triangle base (legs 3,4) \(\times\) height 7 \(\Rightarrow A{=}6,\ V{=}42\)
Derive, don't memorize
unroll curved surfaces into flat rectangles
cylinder's lateral surface \(=2\pi r \times h\)
