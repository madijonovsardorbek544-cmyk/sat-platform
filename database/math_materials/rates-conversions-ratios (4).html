SAT MATH · CHAPTER 10 REV. 01

Rates, Conversions, & Ratios

Conversion Factors · Area & Speed · Average Rate · Ratios

The conversion-factor method for turning any rate into a unit-canceling multiplier, squaring factors for area conversions, handling two units at once for speed, computing average rate of change, and setting up and combining ratios. Techniques first, then worked examples and a 10-question practice set (3 easy · 3 medium · 3 hard · 1 extreme).

01 Techniques
Set up conversion factors so units cancel
\(\text{qty} \times \dfrac{\text{new unit}}{\text{old unit}} = \text{converted qty}\)
\(320 \text{ bikes} \times \dfrac{1\text{ hr}}{20\text{ bikes}} = 16\text{ hr}\)
A rate IS a conversion factor — use it or its reciprocal
\(\dfrac{A}{B} \text{ or } \dfrac{B}{A}\), whichever cancels the unit you have
50 mi/hr → use \(\dfrac{1\text{hr}}{50\text{mi}}\) to convert miles to hours
Square the factor to convert area units
\(1\text{u}_1=k\cdot\text{u}_2 \Rightarrow 1\text{u}_1^2=k^2\cdot\text{u}_2^2\)
\(1\text{m}=3.28\text{ft} \Rightarrow 1\text{m}^2=(3.28)^2\text{ft}^2\)
Convert speeds one unit at a time — length, then time
\(\dfrac{\text{len}_1}{\text{time}_1}\times\dfrac{\text{len}_2}{\text{len}_1}\times\dfrac{\text{time}_1}{\text{time}_2}\)
\(210\tfrac{\text{in}}{\text{s}} \to \tfrac{\text{ft}}{\text{s}} \to \tfrac{\text{ft}}{\text{min}} = 1{,}050\tfrac{\text{ft}}{\text{min}}\)
Average rate of change = change in quantity ÷ elapsed time
\(\text{rate}=\dfrac{Q_2-Q_1}{t_2-t_1}\)
\(\dfrac{360-100}{6-2}=\dfrac{260}{4}=65\)
A ratio is a fraction — cross-multiply to solve
\(a:b = c:d \Rightarrow \dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d} \Rightarrow ad=bc\)
\(y:2.4 = 2.7:3.6 \Rightarrow y=\dfrac{(2.4)(2.7)}{3.6}\)
Combine two ratios through their shared term
\(a{:}b \text{ and } b{:}c \to \text{scale to match } b \to a{:}b{:}c\)
\(1{:}2\) and \(4{:}3\) share pepper → scale to \(2{:}4\) → \(2{:}4{:}3\)
Part-to-whole from a ratio
\(\text{in } a{:}b,\ \text{share of } a = \dfrac{a}{a+b}\times\text{total}\)
ratio \(2{:}4{:}3\) → third part is \(\tfrac{3}{9}\) of the total
"Per" always means divide
\(\text{"A per B"} = \dfrac{A}{B}\)
150 miles in 3 hours \(=\dfrac{150}{3}=50\) mph
