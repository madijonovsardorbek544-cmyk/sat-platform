01 Formulas
"Of" means multiply
\(p\%\text{ of }x = \dfrac{p}{100}x\)
15% of x = 0.15x
Percent increase
\(p\%\text{ greater than }x = \left(1+\dfrac{p}{100}\right)x\)
15% greater than x = 1.15x
Percent decrease
\(p\%\text{ less than }x = \left(1-\dfrac{p}{100}\right)x\)
15% less than x = 0.85x
Reverse translation
read a decimal coefficient back into words
1.15x → "15% greater than x"  ·  0.85x → "15% less than x"
Successive percents
multiply the fractions together
20% of 40% of x = 0.20 × 0.40 × x = 0.08x
Chaining % changes
multiply ALL the factors at once
+20% then −25% then +5% tax = ×1.20×0.75×1.05 — never step-by-step
Percent change formula
\(\dfrac{\text{new} - \text{original}}{\text{original}} \times 100\%\)
always divide by the ORIGINAL value
"Than" marks the original value
in "A% greater/less than B"
B is the base — the denominator in the percent-change formula
You can't just "reverse" a % change
set up an equation instead
This Year = (1+rate) × Last Year → solve for Last Year algebraically
"X times" ≠ "X% greater than"
200 is 400% of 50, but only 300% greater
"greater than" always excludes the original 100%
Multi-relationship chains
translate each sentence into its own equation
then combine/solve algebraically — don't guess
WATCH OUT — Percent change always depends on the starting value. A 30% increase followed by a 30% decrease does NOT bring you back to where you started — you'll end up lower, because the second 30% is taken off a bigger number.
