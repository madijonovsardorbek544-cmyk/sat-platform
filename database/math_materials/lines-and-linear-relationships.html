01 Formulas
Slope between two points
\(m=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\)
\((1,2)\to(4,8):\ m=\dfrac{8-2}{4-1}=2\)
Slope-intercept form
\(y=mx+b\)
\(m=\)slope,\ \(b=y\)-intercept
Point-slope form
\(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\)
\(m=3,\ (1,-2):\ y+2=3(x-1)\)
x-intercept
\(\text{set } y=0,\ \text{solve for } x\)
\(2x+3y=12 \to x=6\)
y-intercept
\(\text{set } x=0,\ \text{solve for } y\)
\(2x+3y=12 \to y=4\)
Parallel lines
\(m_1=m_2\)
\(y=2x+1 \parallel y=2x-5\)
Perpendicular lines
\(m_1\cdot m_2=-1\)
\(m_1=2 \Rightarrow m_2=-\dfrac{1}{2}\)
Horizontal line
\(y=k \quad (\text{slope}=0)\)
through \((0,3) \to y=3\)
Vertical line
\(x=k \quad (\text{slope undefined})\)
through \((3,0) \to x=3\)
Slope straight from standard form
\(Ax+By=C \Rightarrow m=-\dfrac{A}{B}\)
\(6x+9y=18 \to m=-\dfrac{6}{9}=-\dfrac{2}{3}\)
SAT shortcut — not spelled out in this chapter
Parallel / perpendicular from A, B
\(\parallel:\ A_1B_2=A_2B_1 \qquad \perp:\ A_1A_2+B_1B_2=0\)
\(2x+3y=6\) & \(3x-2y=4 \to \perp\)
SAT shortcut — skips converting to y = mx + b
