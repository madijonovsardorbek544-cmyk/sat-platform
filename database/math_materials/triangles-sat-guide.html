01 Formulas
Core Triangle Toolkit
Area of a triangle
\(A=\dfrac12 bh\)
base 14, height 9 \(\Rightarrow A=63\)
Isosceles triangle
2 equal sides \(\Rightarrow\) 2 equal opposite angles
\(AB=AC \Rightarrow \angle B=\angle C\)
Equilateral triangle
all 3 sides equal \(\Rightarrow\) every angle \(=60°\)
\(180° \div 3 = 60°\)
Pythagorean theorem
\(a^2+b^2=c^2\)
legs \(a,b\), hypotenuse \(c\) (always the longest side)
Common Pythagorean triples
3-4-5 · 5-12-13 · 7-24-25 · 8-15-17
6-8-10 is just \(2\times\)(3-4-5) — watch for multiples too
45-45-90 triangle
legs \(x,\ x\) — hypotenuse \(x\sqrt2\)
right isosceles triangle
30-60-90 triangle
shortest \(x\) — longer leg \(x\sqrt3\) — hypotenuse \(2x\)
shortest side is opposite the 30° angle
Triangle congruence theorems
SSS SAS ASA AAS SSA
SSA does NOT prove congruence — more than one triangle can fit
Triangle similarity theorems
AA SAS SSS SSA
AA is by far the most commonly tested — 2 equal angles is always enough
Similar triangle proportions
\(\dfrac{AB}{DE}=\dfrac{AC}{DF}=\dfrac{BC}{EF}\)
corresponding sides share one common ratio

Which Formula Do You Need?
Two sides of a right triangle given, need the third?Pythagorean theorem
A right triangle has a 45° or a 30°/60° angle?Special right triangle ratios
Need to prove two triangles are identical in size?Check SSS, SAS, ASA, or AAS
Same shape, different size?Check AA, SAS, or SSS similarity, then set up a proportion
⚠ A segment cuts across a triangle, parallel to one side?Use the OUTER triangle's real sides in your proportion — never a sub-segment like AD
Corresponding parts trap
\(\dfrac{BD}{BA}=\dfrac{DE}{AC}\)
not \(\dfrac{AD}{DB}=\dfrac{AC}{DE}\) — AD isn't a triangle side
Altitude-to-hypotenuse shortcuts
\(GK^2=FK\cdot KH\)
also: \(\text{Area}=\frac12(\text{leg}_1)(\text{leg}_2)=\frac12(\text{hyp})(\text{altitude})\)
Similarity beyond triangles
any polygon can be similar to another
same 2 rules apply: congruent angles + proportional sides
