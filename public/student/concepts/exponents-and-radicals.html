01 Formulas
Power of one
\(x^1 = x\)
\(3^1 = 3\)
Zero exponent
\(x^0 = 1,\ (x \neq 0)\)
\(7^0 = 1\)
Product rule
\(x^m \cdot x^n = x^{m+n}\)
\(2^3 \cdot 2^4 = 2^7\)
Quotient rule
\(\dfrac{x^m}{x^n} = x^{m-n}\)
\(\dfrac{5^6}{5^2} = 5^4\)
Power of a power
\((x^m)^n = x^{mn}\)
\((2^3)^2 = 2^6\)
Power of a product
\((xy)^m = x^m y^m\)
\((2 \cdot 3)^2 = 2^2 3^2\)
Power of a quotient
\(\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)^m = \dfrac{x^m}{y^m}\)
\(\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}\)
Negative exponent
\(x^{-m} = \dfrac{1}{x^m}\)
\(2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{8}\)
Fractional exponent → root
\(x^{\frac{1}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{x}\)
\(9^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{9} = 3\)
General fractional exponent
\(x^{\frac{m}{n}} = \left(\sqrt[n]{x}\right)^m\)
\(8^{\frac{2}{3}} = 2^2 = 4\)
Product of roots
\(\sqrt{xy} = \sqrt{x}\cdot\sqrt{y}\)
\(\sqrt{4 \cdot 9} = 2 \cdot 3\)
Quotient of roots
\(\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{y}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{y}}\)
\(\sqrt{\dfrac{16}{4}} = \dfrac{4}{2}\)
