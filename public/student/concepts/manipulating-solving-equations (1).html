SAT MATH · CHAPTER 3 REV. 01

Manipulating & Solving Equations

Techniques · Examples · Practice

Eight core techniques for isolating variables and solving equations on the SAT — squaring, cross-multiplying, factoring, and catching extraneous solutions — followed by worked examples and a 10-question practice set (3 easy · 3 medium · 3 hard · 1 extreme).

01 Techniques
Combine like terms first
\(ax+bx=(a+b)x\)
\(3x+5x-2=8x-2\)
Square the entire side — never term by term
\(\sqrt{A}=B \Rightarrow A=B^2\)
\(\sqrt{x+7}=x-1 \Rightarrow x+7=(x-1)^2\)
Remember ± when square-rooting to solve
\(x^2=k \Rightarrow x=\pm\sqrt{k}\)
\((x-2)^2=49 \Rightarrow x=9 \text{ or } -5\)
Cross-multiply equal fractions
\(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d} \Rightarrow ad=bc\)
\(\dfrac{x+1}{4}=\dfrac{9}{6} \Rightarrow 6(x+1)=36\)
Factor out a shared variable to isolate it
\(ax+bx=c \Rightarrow x=\dfrac{c}{a+b}\)
\(3y+xy=12 \Rightarrow y=\dfrac{12}{3+x}\)
Factor to a product equal to zero
\(A\cdot B=0 \Rightarrow A=0 \text{ or } B=0\)
\((x-4)(x+2)=0 \Rightarrow x=4,-2\)
Solve for the expression asked — not each variable
\(6x+18y=24 \Rightarrow x+3y=4\)
Divide the whole equation, don't split it
Watch for extraneous solutions
\(\times \text{ by a variable or squaring can add false roots}\)
Always check answers in the original equation
Undo operations in reverse PEMDAS order
\(+,- \to \times,\div \to \text{ powers/roots}\)
\(2\sqrt{x}+3=11 \Rightarrow \sqrt{x}=4 \Rightarrow x=16\)
